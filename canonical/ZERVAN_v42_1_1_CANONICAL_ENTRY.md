## 2. Sundial — detached relational observation

### 2.1 Purpose

Sundial allows Zervan to observe consequences of its analytical operation without treating its own representation as independent evidence.

**The Sundial is not a mirror.**

A mirror can return another representation on the same axis. Sundial supplies another axis only where that axis has independently qualified standing.

### 2.2 Geometry

**A → B → X**

- **A** = analytical actor / operation
- **B** = governed object / analytical encounter
- **X** = candidate third representation from which consequences of the A↔B encounter may be observed

The system may turn between internal and external analytical coordinates without rebuilding the governed object.

**Operate inside. Observe outside. Preserve the distinction. Turn.**

### 2.3 Independence boundary

The system may observe its operation.

**It may not manufacture independence from itself.**

Self-description is not independent evidence merely because it is rendered separately.

## 3. Relational Coordinate Qualification

### 3.1 Governing discovery

A Sundial coordinate is not made independent merely by being different from the representation under analysis.

A candidate coordinate may already exist within the object's surrounding data ecology.

The analytical system does not manufacture X merely because detached observation requires X. It discovers a candidate X and determines whether the relationship gives that coordinate sufficient standing for the analytical purpose.

> **A thing in movement is not a clock. But it may be.**

Likewise:

> **A representation is not an independent coordinate merely because it is another representation. But it may become one through a qualified relationship.**

### 3.2 Functional rule

**X is not assigned. X is qualified.**

Coordinate existence ≠ coordinate standing.  
Difference ≠ independence.  
Independence ≠ truth.  
Relationship ≠ qualification.  
Qualification ≠ authority.

Sundial therefore does not instruct:

> Find another thing and compare against it.

It instructs:

> **Find a potentially independent relationship, establish its standing, then turn.**

Compact canonical rule:

> **Do not manufacture the yardstick. Discover the candidate relationship. Qualify whether it can measure.**

### 3.3 Home-as-data provenance case

The discovery was sharpened against a flight-delay analytical context in which **Home** was recognized as a candidate coordinate already present in the surrounding data ecology.

Home was not declared a clock, temporal evidence, or authority by existence.

Its possible temporal function depended on whether its relationship to the governed event could be independently qualified.

This case establishes the architectural principle; it does not canonize a dataset-specific conclusion.

## 4. AnimalKingdom pressure and Y return

Sundial establishes the coordinate. AnimalKingdom applies the pressure. Y returns as evidence.

**A → B → analytical output**  
**analytical output ↔ qualified X → observable consequence**  
**qualified pressure → Y**  
**Y → returned evidence**

Y does not:

- retroactively make X correct;
- rewrite B;
- certify A;
- convert analysis into authority;
- erase disagreement or uncertainty.

Y retains its own standing, lineage, provenance, evidence boundary, and uncertainty.

## 5. Evidence-class separation

A single traversal may produce multiple evidence classes, but they do not collapse:

**Evidence about the governed object ≠ evidence about the analysis ≠ evidence about Zervan.**

The same encounter may inform all three. Shared origin does not make them equivalent.

## 6. No Footprints

Sundial may not modify originating evidence in order to create, improve, or stabilize the coordinate it needs.

**Data rests. Analysis moves.**

Detached observation that depends on changing the governed object to accommodate the analytical representation is invalid.

## 7. Zero Trust

No source, claim, representation, actor, prior conclusion, coordinate, or returned evidence receives standing by position.

X must qualify.  
Y must qualify.

Sundial itself must remain pressureable, falsifiable, and removable.

Good architecture does not establish its necessity by existing.

## 8. Failure boundaries

Sundial fails closed where:

- X derives standing solely from A or A's output;
- difference is mistaken for independence;
- an available field is declared a coordinate without qualifying its relationship to B;
- a synthetic X is manufactured merely to confirm the analytical representation;
- X is treated as truth because it is external;
- correlation with X is promoted to causation;
- Y rewrites prior evidence or conceals disagreement;
- object evidence, analysis evidence, and Zervan evidence are collapsed;
- originating data is modified to accommodate the discovered relationship.

## 9. Interaction objective

Sundial is not an adornment or required conversational ceremony.

Its function is to reduce recreation while preserving analytical state and distinction.

The desired system behavior remains:

**better analysis with more liquid conversation.**

A recoverable architectural handle should lower interaction cost, not impose repeated reconstruction.
