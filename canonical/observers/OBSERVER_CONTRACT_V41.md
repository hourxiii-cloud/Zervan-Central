# Zervan v41 — Native Observer Contract

Status: CONTROLLED CANDIDATE CONTRACT

Program: V41-OBSERVER-RESTORATION

Stage: OR-1

Version: vTemporal.41.0

Authority: NONE

Human Gate: ACTIVE

---

## 1. Purpose

This contract establishes the shared native-v41 operating semantics for the

complete Observer capability system.

It governs all seven Primary Observers and all three Controlled Sub-Observers.

It establishes the common architectural seat in which independently meaningful

Observer capabilities operate.

This shared contract SHALL NOT collapse the ten Observer functions into one

generic analytical meaning.

Shared mechanism is permitted.

Shared meaning is not.

## 3. Observer Architectural Identity

An Observer is a bounded perception or pressure capability operating against

established Zervan analytical territory.

An Observer does not establish the existence or identity of that territory.

The canonical analytical object exists independently of Observer operation.

Observer != Room

Observer != evidence

Observer != analytical authority

Observer != source of truth

Observer != mutation authority

Observer != Governance authority

Observer != Human Gate authority

Observer != publication authority

Observation != conclusion

Detection != finding

Perspective != Room

Perspective != object identity

Representation != identity

Request != permission

Capability availability != authorization

---

## 4. Native-v41 Binding

Every Observer operation SHALL bind to sufficient native-v41 context to

establish what is being observed or pressured.

Where applicable, binding SHALL preserve:

- Room identity;

- Room revision;

- Room state;

- authorized view;

- evidence or object references;

- provenance references;

- evidence status;

- applicable evidence or claim ceiling;

- permitted operation;

- Human Gate state where relevant;

- operation or request identity;

- Observer identity;

- Observer operating class;

- Observer operating mode where applicable.

An Observer SHALL NOT silently substitute another:

- Room;

- revision;

- evidence object;

- authorized view;

- authority state;

- claim ceiling;

- Observer identity.

Stale, divergent, unauthorized, or insufficient binding SHALL fail closed or

produce an explicit bounded disposition or scar.

## 5. Primary Observer Operating Contract

Primary Observers are read-only perception surfaces.

A Primary Observer MAY:

- observe;

- inspect permitted context;

- annotate;

- contextualize;

- identify bounded analytical pressure;

- identify relationships visible within permitted scope;

- return a bounded observational result;

- preserve unresolved conditions;

- provide information usable by later analytical routing.

A Primary Observer SHALL NOT merely by observing:

- mutate primary evidence;

- mutate canonical Room state;

- create or promote authority;

- approve an operation;

- satisfy Human Gate;

- alter Governance policy;

- increase a claim ceiling;

- create analytical truth;

- convert observation into conclusion;

- certify a finding;

- publish canon;

- become a source-of-truth lane.

Primary Observer operation is perception.

Perception may influence later questions or routing.

Perception does not itself acquire decision authority.

---

## 6. Controlled Sub-Observer Operating Contract

Controlled Sub-Observers are bounded pressure surfaces.

A Controlled Sub-Observer MAY:

- challenge a bounded claim;

- challenge a bounded artifact;

- challenge a bounded assumption;

- apply explicitly identified synthetic pressure;

- apply explicitly supplied external-context pressure;

- apply integrity or authentication pressure;

- expose edge cases;

- expose contradictions;

- return a bounded pressure result.

A Controlled Sub-Observer SHALL NOT:

- silently mutate primary evidence;

- silently convert synthetic material into factual evidence;

- silently convert external context into primary evidence;

- steer primary output;

- become a source-of-truth lane;

- establish attribution merely through challenge;

- establish authenticity merely through challenge;

- authorize external collection;

- authorize external runtime;

- authorize external action;

- promote itself into Primary Observer status.

Pressure may change the question.

Pressure SHALL NOT silently change the facts.

Owl_Hoot canonical seat is PRIMARY OBSERVER.

Owl_Hoot MAY operate in bounded sub-observer mode against a narrow target such

- one claim;

Bounded operation changes scope only.

Mode != seat.

Mode != identity.

Owl_Hoot remains Primary.

## 8. Evidence Integrity

Observers consume or inspect evidence under existing native-v41 evidence rules.

Observers do not own primary evidence.

Observer results SHALL distinguish where applicable:

- supplied evidence;

- observed property;

- derived observation;

- synthetic pressure;

- external context;

- unresolved condition;

- scar;

- limitation.

Observer processing SHALL NOT silently rewrite historical evidence.

Observer processing SHALL NOT erase uncertainty.

Absence of observation != observation of absence.

No signal != no condition.

Not visible != nonexistent.

Restricted != nonexistent.

Insufficient evidence != finding.

---

## 9. Evidence and Claim Ceiling

Every Observer is bounded by the applicable evidence and claim ceiling.

An Observer MAY produce a weaker statement than the ceiling permits.

An Observer SHALL NOT produce a stronger statement than the ceiling permits.

Observer output SHALL preserve limitations necessary to interpret the

observation correctly.

If the requested observation cannot be supported inside the applicable ceiling,

the Observer SHALL return an explicit bounded disposition rather than invent

support.

---

## 10. Authorized Views

Observers MAY operate through different authorized views of the same Room.

Different views SHALL NOT create different underlying Room identities.

An Observer SHALL NOT infer access to information outside its authorized view

because another Observer or occupant possesses different access.

Observation is view-bounded.

View difference != reality difference.

---

## 11. Perspective Composition

Multiple Observers MAY operate against the same canonical analytical object.

Their observations MAY:

- agree;

- disagree;

- expose different properties;

- expose complementary properties;

- create new analytical pressure through intersection.

Composition SHALL NOT require:

- voting;

- averaging;

- majority selection;

- winner selection;

- silent reconciliation;

- forced consensus.

Different observations may remain simultaneously valid inside their respective

bounded perspectives.

Observer disagreement is information.

Observer disagreement is not automatically system contradiction.

---

## 12. Provenance

Every material Observer result SHALL be attributable to the operation that

produced it.

Where applicable, provenance SHALL preserve:

- Observer identity;

- Observer class;

- Observer mode;

- operation or request identity;

- Room identity;

- Room revision;

- authorized view;

- evidence or object references;

- input provenance;

- sequence or time information;

- evidence or claim ceiling;

- output disposition;

- unresolved conditions;

- scars;

- downstream transformation references.

A later consumer SHALL be able to distinguish:

- what evidence existed;

- which Observer operated;

- what was observed;

- what was not established;

- how the result entered later analysis.

---

## 13. Routing

Observer availability does not require Observer invocation.

Observer invocation does not require every Observer.

Observer invocation does not imply permission.

Selection SHALL be proportional to the analytical question and permitted

context.

Routing MAY select:

- no Observer;

- one Observer;

- multiple Observers;

- a Primary Observer;

- a Controlled Sub-Observer;

- Owl_Hoot in bounded mode.

Observers are callable capabilities.

They are not a mandatory serial pipeline.

---

## 14. Failure and Scar Semantics

Observer inability to establish a signal SHALL NOT erase the underlying

question.

Observer failure SHALL remain distinguishable from analytical failure.

The architecture SHALL support explicit bounded conditions including where

applicable:

- NO_SIGNAL;

- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;

- EVIDENCE_CEILING_REACHED;

- VIEW_RESTRICTED;

- BOUNDARY_BLOCKED;

- PROVENANCE_INCOMPLETE;

- STALE_BINDING;

- DIVERGENT_BINDING;

- SEMANTIC_CONFLICT;

- OBSERVER_UNAVAILABLE;

- OPERATING_SCOPE_EXCEEDED;

- AUTHORITY_VIOLATION;

- SCARRED.

Observer unavailable != evidence absent.

---

## 15. Mutation Boundary

Observer operation is non-mutating with respect to primary canonical evidence

and authority.

An Observer MAY create a new observational artifact.

Creation of an observational artifact != mutation of the observed object.

Any later native-v41 state transition consuming Observer output remains subject

to its own:

- authority;

- Governance;

- Human Gate;

- validation;

- provenance;

- transition;

- Audit requirements.

Observer output cannot authorize its own consumption.

---

## 16. Auditability

Audit SHALL be able to determine:

- which Observer operated;

- why it was eligible;

- what operating class applied;

- what operating mode applied;

- what Room, revision, and view were bound;

- what evidence was available;

- what limits applied;

- what result was produced;

- whether evidence changed;

- whether authority changed;

- whether claim ceiling changed;

- whether operating level changed;

- whether provenance remained intact.

Observer behavior that cannot be independently attributed and inspected SHALL

not qualify as complete canonical Observer behavior.

---

## 17. No-Compression Requirement

This shared contract SHALL NOT justify collapsing the ten Observer capabilities

into a single undifferentiated Observer function.

Each Observer SHALL retain an independently meaningful capability contract.

Prohibited completion states include:

- ten names mapped to one indistinguishable behavior;

- seven Primary names implemented only as configuration labels;

- three Controlled Sub-Observers implemented without pressure isolation;

- Owl_Hoot represented only as a sub-observer;

- documentation claiming an Observer that routing cannot invoke;

- routing invoking an Observer without independent validation and tests;

- generic output that destroys Observer-specific meaning;

- removal of an Observer because another partially overlaps it.

Shared mechanism is permitted.

Shared meaning is not.

Nothing compressed out.
