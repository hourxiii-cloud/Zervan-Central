## 1. PURPOSE

Determine whether a common relational geometry can describe constrained
flow, pressure development, Vortex formation, Backflow/Burst behavior,
and radically different large-scale interacting systems without
collapsing scale, force origin, agency, path geometry, or distinct forms
of motion into one another.

This CR does not presume that one geometry will survive the comparison.
A qualified split is a valid outcome.

## 2. DEVELOPMENTAL QUESTION

Can the retrieved Drain / Pipe / Flow / Vortex / Backflow-Burst family
and the cosmology counterobject be composed into a lossless relational
geometry while preserving the distinctions already earned by each
object?

Candidate aggregate question:

> Can a common relational geometry describe constrained flow, internally
> developing pressure, Vortex formation, Backflow/Burst behavior, and
> radically different large-scale interacting systems without collapsing
> scale, force origin, agency, or distinct forms of motion into one
> another?

## 3. INPUT OBJECTS

### A. Drain / Pipe / Flow

Constrained path geometry in which flow is conditioned by available path
and constraint rather than capability alone.

### B. Pre-contact relational field

Interaction may precede contact. Contact does not necessarily establish
interaction inception. Emergent structure does not establish an agent.

### C. Vortex

A materially distinct exhibition from simple expected/constrained
directional flow.

Recovered possible trajectory:

`CONSTRAINED / EXPECTED FLOW → INDIFFERENCE → VORTEX → ISSUE → BACKFLOW → BURST`

Possible, not mandatory.

### D. Backflow / Back-pressure / Burst

Backflow may be active negative flow, may be volatile, and may burst. A
Vortex-like exhibition may later validate as Backflow. Exact historical
Back-pressure↔Backflow relation remains unresolved.

### E. Force-Origin Economy

`ΔPRESSURE RELATION ≠ NECESSARILY ΔEXTERNAL FORCE`

`INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT ≠ INTERNAL ORIGIN`

Later pressure development may remain conditioned by earlier
force/qualifier relations without requiring a newly introduced external
force at every transition.

### F. Cosmology counterobject

Recovered developmental trace:

`DRINKS / DRAIN CONSTRICTION` → `COUNTEROBJECT: COSMOS` →
`ROTATING PLANETS REJECTED AS INSUFFICIENT VORTEX COMPARISON` →
`COLLIDING SOLAR SYSTEMS SELECTED`

Recovered workflow:

`BUILD → COMPARE → TEST → TEST → INTEGRATE → CR`

The cosmology object pressures the geometry across scale. It does not
establish identity across scale.

constant?

origin?

### Q4 --- VORTEX

and Burst?

### Q5 --- BACKFLOW

reversal appearance, or Vortex?

Backflow without synonym-by-convenience.

### Q7 --- BURST

### Q8 --- AGENCY

compression?

## 9. HOSTILE PRESSURES

The CR must reject at least these failure modes:

-   **Universal-flow attack:** everything becomes Flow.
-   **Universal-vortex attack:** any nonlinear movement becomes Vortex.
-   **Reverse-equals-backflow attack:** any reversal is declared
    Backflow.
-   **Pressure-equals-force attack:** every pressure change requires a
    newly introduced force.
-   **Internal-equals-origin attack:** internally developing relation is
    declared internally originated.
-   **Burst inevitability attack:** Backflow is assumed to terminate in
    Burst.
-   **Agency manufacture attack:** organized behavior is treated as
    evidence of an actor.
-   **Scale flattening attack:** drain and cosmos are treated as the
    same object because some relations rhyme.
-   **Cosmology prestige attack:** large-scale physics is allowed to
    overwrite the smaller object's earned distinctions.
-   **Historical reconstruction attack:** missing primitive definitions
    are generated and misrepresented as recovered history.
-   **CR promotion attack:** developmental coherence is treated as
    canonical standing.
