## Frozen proposition
An analytical object o may be represented by a typed six-coordinate analytical tuple:

A(o) = (I, R, S, T, X, V)

I = Identity: persistent object identifier and continuity evidence.
R = Relationship: typed relationships, directionality, references, provenance.
S = Space: location within an explicitly declared analytical reference frame.
T = Time: observation, event, and transformation clocks and ordering.
X = Transformation: input/output state changes, lineage, invariants.
V = Verification: evidence, tests, confidence, receipts, unresolved standing.

These are typed analytical dimensions, not six independent Euclidean axes. Six arbitrary points in 3D space do not establish this coordinate system. The tuple is not automatically a globally unique address.

## Architectural proposition
AZIMUTH is a candidate object-addressing layer for analytical subnetting, ML and AI. Its purpose is to make identity, relations, spatial and temporal context, transformation history, and verification inspectable without conflating them. Subnetting distributes analytical work, not underlying evidence or identity. Replication is not independent observation.
