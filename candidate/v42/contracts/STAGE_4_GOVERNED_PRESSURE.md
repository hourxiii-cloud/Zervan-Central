# Stage 4 — Qualified Endogenous Provocation

Stage 4 is a composite governed protocol, not a silent extension of canonical
AnimalKingdom. AnimalKingdom may be an eligible carrier only where its canonical v41
contract accepts the exact mission and inputs.

## Control chain

1. Trigger qualification determines pressure eligibility.
2. Pressure routing selects minimum sufficient force.
3. Carrier admissibility verifies exact contract fit.
4. Execution authorization requires a positive current source.
5. Envelope enforcement bounds all in-run motion.
6. Termination distinguishes completion, non-finding, stop, failure, abort, and contamination.
7. Clean return preserves the same object and attaches pressure history.
8. Evidence qualification determines what the returned result supports.

No control may impersonate, override, or silently recompute another.

## Force law

Need determines force. Question determines mission. Evidence determines escalation.

Smaller admissible sufficiency stops escalation. Escalation requires newly established
evidence of a specific capability gap.

`ELIGIBLE != AUTHORIZED`  
`ROUTED != EXECUTED`  
`CAPABILITY_SUFFICIENCY != CARRIER_ADMISSIBILITY`

`STOP != FAILURE`  
`ABORT != CONTAMINATION`  
`RETURN != RECONSTRUCTION`  
`PRESSURE_OUTPUT != QUALIFIED_EVIDENCE`
