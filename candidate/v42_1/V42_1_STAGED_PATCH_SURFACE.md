## Purpose
This patch extends the four existing v42 stages. It does not replace, compress, reconcile, or silently reinterpret them. Originating data remains untouched. Analytical state, qualification, interpretation, and representation remain distinguishable from the object interrogated.

## 1. Same-Data Referential Anchoring / No Footprints
Originating data remains originating data. Analytical movement SHALL NOT require mutation, annotation, correction, enrichment, or interpretive write-back to originating evidence.

Different analytical actors, perspectives, questions, operations, and representations MAY move around the same data while retaining demonstrable reference to that same data.

Interpretation may move. Perspective may move. Significance may move. Attention may move. Reference SHALL NOT silently drift.

Integrity establishes what was supplied and whether state changed detectably. Provenance establishes descent and route. Neither establishes truth.

**Core constraint: No footprints on originating data.**

## 2. Interpretive Divergence Without Referential Drift
Independent interpretations MAY diverge without requiring reconciliation into one interpretation, provided each remains attributable to the same preserved data relationship and its own qualified analytical state.

One-to-Many permits independent movement around the same object. Many-to-One permits independent contributions to return without consensus collapse.

Common reference SHALL NOT require common conclusion. A report, summary, role perspective, or downstream interpretation SHALL NOT substitute for the originating data relationship.

## 3. Zero Trust Analytical Standing
No analytical actor, source, claim, classification, prior conclusion, relationship, representation, role, or system output receives implicit analytical authority merely because of position or origin.

Data is data. Source assertions remain source assertions. Analysis remains analysis. Agreement does not establish truth. Disagreement does not establish error. Human approval does not convert evidence into truth.

Standing is earned through interrogation and qualification and remains bounded by evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and the conditions under which it survived pressure.

Enterprise access control remains owned by the authoritative access-control environment. Zervan SHALL NOT duplicate access authority merely to perform analysis. Role may change authorized data availability; role SHALL NOT inherently change analytical capability.

## 4. Null Space
**Null Space — qualified release from attention until plausible action warrants return.**

A valid engagement producing no return sufficient to justify continued attention MAY enter Null Space while preserving coordinate, engagement conditions, evidence boundary, applied force, return, release basis, integrity, lineage, and provenance.

Null does not mean absence, failure, rejection, permanent resolution, or permission to erase prior engagement. No additional analytical force is warranted solely because no qualifying information returned.

Return requires plausible action, not mere possibility. Rehydration SHALL restore minimum sufficient prior analytical state and compare prior Null standing against the return provocation.

Return outcomes MAY include PERSIST, BREAK, or RESHAPE. Rehydration does not itself imply force expansion.

## 5. Earned Significance / Dynamic Hydration
**Attention follows earned significance through time.**

**Do not predict importance. Preserve enough to measure it when it arrives.**

**Relevance is temporal. Standing is durable. Residency is conditional.**

Persistent state does not require persistent attention or persistent hydration. Information SHALL NOT remain actively resident merely because it was once relevant.

Low current significance does not establish permanent insignificance. Release is controlled non-residency, not forgetting, rejection, compression-out, or loss of provenance.

As qualified relationships, recurrence, temporal behavior, independent intersections, qualified absence, contradiction, or other measured consequence increase significance, hydration MAY increase as warranted. As present necessity declines, residency MAY be reduced while standing and recoverability persist.

The durable analytical field MAY grow while the active field remains bounded by present earned significance.

## 6. AnimalKingdom — Necessity by Consequence / Removal Testing
Analytical force follows qualified need, not analytical enthusiasm.

Capability specialization MAY emerge locally without architecture predefining permanent specialist identity. Function precedes durable identity. Recurrence and demonstrated requirement MAY justify promotion; novelty alone SHALL NOT.

When useful specialization is unknown within qualified territory, bounded multiplicity MAY be used as discovery pressure. **Flood to find specificity. Collapse when specificity appears.**

Known territory SHALL NOT require reconstruction merely to reestablish position. **Persistent field. Ephemeral force.**

A claimed necessary capability MAY be removed under preserved conditions to test necessity. The system SHALL NOT silently compensate for removal merely to preserve the necessity claim.

If removal produces no material consequence, the necessity claim loses standing under those conditions. If removal produces a distinctive failure or degradation silhouette, that consequence MAY qualify the capability's actual operating boundary. If another capability fills the hole, substitution or redundancy has been discovered.

**The animals do not establish their necessity by existing. The terrain earns them.**

## 7. Same-Traversal Processing and Qualification
Zervan MAY process an object while simultaneously producing evidence about the fitness of the analytical operation used to process it.

Processing and testing MAY be geometrically coincident while remaining epistemically distinct.

Evidence about the object SHALL NOT be confused with evidence about Zervan's operation.

One traversal may yield distinguishable evidence classes:
- object-side observations, relationships, propositions, or null returns;
- operation-side evidence concerning necessity, sufficiency, excess, specialization, substitution, failure, or fitness;
- Human-Gate pressure concerning analytical standing.

Failure is telemetry. Retraction is retained analytical state. A failed proposition SHALL NOT require mutation of originating data.

Testing is not necessarily downstream of processing. Analytical movement itself MAY create the test surface.

**The Room is both terrain and proving ground.**
**Zervan processes the data while the data pressures Zervan.**

## 8. Möbius Internal / External Geometry
Zervan may generate an operation, experience its consequence, and expose that operation itself as something available for interrogation without confusing the operation with the originating object it interrogated.

Internal and external are not required to be separate sequential modes. They MAY be continuously reachable analytical coordinates within preserved lineage.

Zervan may move between:
- What does this operation tell us about the data?
- What does this encounter tell us about the operation?

and return without rebuilding the field.

Object pressure may test a proposition. Encounter pressure may test capability. Human Gate may test analytical standing. These SHALL remain independently attributable even when arising during the same traversal.

Möbius supports continuous movement between operating upon the field and interrogating the operation itself while preserving object identity, state relationship, integrity, provenance, and evidence-class distinction.

## 9. Cross-Surface Composition
These developments compose with, rather than replace, the four existing v42 stages.

- One-to-Many / Many-to-One permits independent movement and coherent return without interpretive collapse.
- Möbius permits movement between analytical coordinates without object substitution or reconstruction.
- Harmony may inspect agreement, disagreement, resonance, and changing relational significance without converting relationships into truth or authority.
- Governed Pressure permits capability to be exercised, challenged, removed, specialized, collapsed, rehydrated, or released while Human Gate remains active.
- No Footprints preserves the originating data boundary across all movement.
- Zero Trust Analytical Standing prevents inherited epistemic privilege.
- Null Space and Dynamic Hydration bound attention and residency without destroying recoverability.
- AnimalKingdom supplies necessary force while allowing necessity itself to be tested.
- Same-traversal qualification makes operation a live proving ground without collapsing evidence classes.

## 10. Non-Claims / Locks
This patch does NOT establish:
- originating data is true because it is preserved;
- provenance establishes truth;
- agreement establishes truth;
- Zervan analysis establishes decision authority;
- Human Gate approval establishes factual truth;
- reports or representations become the originating object;
- all enterprise roles must share an interpretation;
- every capability must remain resident;
- every null return warrants further force;
- every specialization deserves durable identity;
- processing and testing become the same evidence class;
- internal/external movement permits self-certification;
- v42.1 is canonical merely because this staged patch is approved.
