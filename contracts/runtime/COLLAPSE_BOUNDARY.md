# R4-B — Collapse Boundary

Status: CONTROLLED CANDIDATE CONTRACT
Ring: R4-B
Version: vTemporal.41.0
Promotion State: CANDIDATE

---

## 0. Purpose

R4-B defines native-v41 Collapse Boundary.

Collapse is an evidence-driven signal and refinement event within the Room
Object.

Collapse records why the object can no longer support the prior range of
possibilities at the current evidence ceiling.

Collapse is not merely compression.

Collapse is not summary.

Collapse is not deletion.

Collapse is not creation of another world.

---

## 1. Dependency

R4-B consumes:

- complete Rings 1 through 3;
- R4-A Restriction / Constriction;
- canonical Room Object identity;
- current representation;
- attributable evidence;
- current evidence boundary;
- current evidence ceiling;
- Cartography;
- provenance;
- Stick continuity.

R4-B does not redefine those substrates.

---

## 2. Collapse Questions

Every valid Collapse Boundary MUST answer:

- Why did collapse become necessary?
- What signal triggered it?
- Which possibilities were removed?
- Which possibilities were retained?
- Which possibilities were deferred?
- Which representation exposed the collapse signal?
- What recreation or re-expansion considerations remain applicable now?
- What invariants must survive?
- What route returns to the pre-collapse state?

Collapse without these answers is incomplete.

- collapse time.

---

## 4. Collapse Identity

Native-v41 `collapse_boundary_id` is deterministic SHA-512 over the complete
Collapse Boundary except its own ID.

A material change to trigger, evidence, possibility disposition,
representation, uncertainty, frontier, evidence ceiling, or return route
changes Collapse Boundary identity.

Collapse Boundary identity is not Room Object identity.

Collapse Boundary identity is not State Root.

---

## 5. One Room Object

Collapse occurs within one Room Object.

Collapse does not replace the Room.

Collapse does not create a private copy.

Collapse does not create a new object merely because the supported possibility
range changed.

Collapse != new Room.

---

## 6. Evidence Driven

Collapse requires attributable governing evidence.

Collapse cannot occur solely because:

- one explanation is aesthetically cleaner;
- a perspective prefers one answer;
- an observer is tired of uncertainty;
- a score is high;
- repetition creates pressure to converge.

Evidence drives collapse.

Preference does not.

---

## 7. Possibility Partition

The pre-collapse possibility set MUST be partitioned into:

- removed possibilities;
- retained possibilities;
- deferred possibilities.

Every removed, retained, or deferred possibility MUST originate in the
pre-collapse possibility set.

A possibility MUST NOT occupy more than one disposition simultaneously.

The union of removed, retained, and deferred possibilities MUST equal the
pre-collapse possibility set.

Collapse MUST NOT silently lose a prior possibility.

---

## 8. Retained Possibilities

Retained possibilities remain currently supported after Collapse.

Retained != proven beyond the evidence ceiling.

Retained != canonical truth.

Retained != decision.

---

## 9. Removed Possibilities

Removed possibilities are no longer supportable under the current evidence and
evidence ceiling.

Removed != deleted.

Removed != historically nonexistent.

Removed != impossible under all future evidence.

Removal basis remains reconstructible.

---

## 10. Deferred Possibilities

Deferred possibilities remain unresolved or unavailable for current support
determination.

Deferred != removed.

Deferred != retained.

Deferred != absent.

A deferred possibility preserves why determination could not be completed.

---

## 11. Surviving Uncertainty

Collapse MUST preserve surviving uncertainty.

Collapse is not permission to manufacture certainty.

A single retained possibility may still carry uncertainty under the current
evidence ceiling.

One surviving possibility != absolute truth.

---

## 12. Evidence Boundary

Collapse MUST remain inside the applicable evidence boundary.

Excluded evidence MUST NOT silently enter the collapse basis.

Restricted evidence MUST NOT be treated as absent.

Collapse cannot broaden evidence access.

---

## 13. Evidence Ceiling

Collapse occurs at an explicit evidence ceiling.

The boundary records why the prior range can no longer stand at that ceiling.

Collapse does not raise the evidence ceiling merely because possibilities were
removed.

Fewer possibilities != greater authority.

---

## 14. Source Representation

Collapse records which representation or representations exposed the collapse
signal.

Representation may expose evidence.

Representation does not create truth authority.

Perspective != authority.

---

## 15. Preserved Invariants

Collapse MUST explicitly preserve applicable invariants, including:

- Room Object identity;
- Origin continuity;
- provenance;
- representation history;
- evidence lineage;
- Cartographic continuity;
- Stick continuity;
- return route.

Collapse MUST NOT erase the structure required to understand the analytical
journey.

---

## 16. Newly Available Frontier

Collapse may expose a newly available analytical frontier.

A new frontier may represent:

- newly discriminable terrain;
- newly reachable evidence;
- a new bounded question;
- a distinct-object candidate.

New frontier != new Room.

---

## 17. New Room Availability

A new Room Object becomes available only when collapse and evidence establish a
genuinely distinct object.

A new perspective does not justify a new Room.

An emotional state does not justify a new Room.

A stakeholder does not justify a new Room.

A discipline does not justify a new Room.

A rendering does not justify a new Room.

Distinct object requires the existing distinct-object controls.

Collapse alone != distinct-object establishment.

---

## 18. Re-Expansion

Collapse preserves re-expansion considerations.

Re-expansion may become justified when new evidence changes what can be
supported.

Re-expansion does not erase the historical Collapse Boundary.

History accumulates.

Re-expansion != reconstruction of an approximate Room.

---

## 19. Return Route

Every Collapse Boundary preserves a route back to the pre-collapse state.

The route supports reconstructibility, replay, audit, and challenge.

Return route != automatic rollback authority.

Reconstructible != reversible without authorization.

Collapse != Cartography deletion.

## 32. Failure Conditions

R4-B MUST reject:

- collapse without governing evidence;
- a possibility not present in the pre-collapse set;
- overlap among removed, retained, and deferred sets;
- silent omission of a pre-collapse possibility;
- lost surviving uncertainty;
- lost provenance;
- lost representation history;
- lost return route;
- evidence-boundary broadening;
- evidence-ceiling promotion by collapse alone;
- object-identity substitution;
- perspective-based new-Room creation;
- compression-out substitution;
- silent lifecycle mutation;
- silent occupancy mutation;
- silent formation selection;
- Landing, Reflight, or Replay semantics pulled forward.
