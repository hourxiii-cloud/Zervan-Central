## 0. Purpose

R5-F binds Raven representation to the Human Gate boundary for
authority-bearing movement.

Raven may prepare representation.

Raven may prepare a publication-ready report.

Raven may prepare an action-bearing movement request.

Raven does not authorize those movements.

Human Gate controls authority-bearing movement.

Human Gate decision != execution.

## 2. Human Gate Boundary

Human Gate preserves the boundary between analysis and authority-bearing action.

Zervan may:

- analyze;
- route;
- classify candidates;
- preserve provenance;
- prepare reports;
- prepare movement packets;
- prepare decision material.

Zervan does not authorize authority-bearing action by itself.

Explicit human approval is required for authority-bearing movement.

## 5. Transition Request

Human Gate evaluates one explicitly named requested transition.

The transition request MUST identify:

- transition class;
- transition target;
- Raven representation being reviewed;
- upstream lineage;
- applicable MC admissibility;
- applicable governance constraints;
- requested scope.

One Human Gate approval applies only to the explicitly requested transition.

Approval for one transition MUST NOT silently authorize another.

Unknown transition class MUST NOT default to approved.

## 8. Human Gate Decision

Native R5-F records exactly one decision state:

- `PENDING`;
- `APPROVED`;
- `DENIED`.

`PENDING` means no authority-bearing movement is authorized.

`APPROVED` means the explicitly named transition is authorized to proceed
through its separately governed execution path.

`DENIED` means the explicitly named transition is not authorized.

No decision state may be inferred from silence.

No default approval exists.

An `APPROVED` decision MUST bind:

- exact transition class;
- exact transition target;
- exact Raven representation;
- exact Room identity;
- exact evidence boundary;
- exact evidence ceiling;
- exact approval scope;
- human decision reference;
- decision timestamp or attributable decision-time reference.

Approval MUST NOT silently expand beyond those bindings.

Approval scope != general authority.
