## Purpose

R8-B defines the machine-readable and human-readable semantics of a Human Gate
promotion decision.

R8-B defines a decision contract.

R8-B does not create a decision.

R8-B does not grant authorization.

R8-B does not execute promotion.

R8-B does not merge branches.

R8-B does not mutate canonical state.

Decision != execution.

Approval != execution.

Receipt != authority.

## Decision States

A Promotion Decision MUST use exactly one decision:

APPROVE

REJECT

DEFER

No other decision state is valid.

## APPROVE

APPROVE means Human Gate explicitly authorizes the exact candidate bound by the
decision record to proceed to the separately governed promotion execution path.

APPROVE does not itself perform promotion.

APPROVE does not itself merge candidate/v41-complete into main.

APPROVE does not itself make v41 canonical.

APPROVE does not itself enable external runtime.

APPROVE does not itself enable external action.

APPROVE does not itself allow system population.

APPROVE authorizes only the exact scope recorded by the decision.

## REJECT

REJECT means the proposed promotion may not proceed.

The candidate remains preserved.

REJECT does not erase Ring 7 readiness evidence.

REJECT does not erase provenance.

REJECT does not mutate main.

A future promotion attempt requires a new governed decision.

## DEFER

DEFER means no promotion authorization is granted at this time.

The candidate remains preserved.

The reason for deferral must remain attributable.

DEFER is not rejection.

DEFER is not approval.

DEFER preserves the Human Gate for a later governed decision.

## Candidate Identity

candidate_branch MUST be:

candidate/v41-complete

candidate_commit MUST identify the exact Git commit being decided upon.

The decision does not float with branch HEAD.

A later change to candidate/v41-complete does not inherit prior approval.

Candidate identity must be rebound through the governed path.

## Target Identity

target_branch MUST be:

main

The target identifies the proposed canonical destination.

Target identity does not prove mutation occurred.

## Decision Scope

decision_scope MUST explicitly state what the Human Gate decision covers.

Decision scope may not silently expand after the decision is recorded.

Execution outside decision scope is unauthorized.

## Decision Actor

decision_actor identifies the human authority making the decision.

It must not be generated by a validator.

It must not be inferred from repository ownership.

It must not be inferred from commit authorship.

Human identity must be explicitly supplied at decision time.

## Decision Timestamp

decision_timestamp records when the Human Gate decision was made.

Timestamp != authorization by itself.

Timestamp != execution.

## Rationale

rationale records the stated basis for the decision.

A rationale may be concise.

A rationale may not be silently invented by automation.

## Provenance

provenance binds the decision to the evidence used for the decision.

At minimum the governed decision path is expected to preserve reference to:

- R7-N aggregate closure;
- R8-A Human Gate / Promotion Boundary;
- R8-B Promotion Decision Contract;
- exact candidate commit;
- target branch.

Provenance != endorsement.

## Authority Boundary

Before a decision instance exists:

Authority = NONE.

Human Gate = ACTIVE.

Human Gate Authorization = NOT_GRANTED.

The existence of this contract does not change authority.

A schema does not change authority.

A validator does not change authority.

A test does not change authority.

## Decision Versus Authorization Receipt

R8-B defines decision semantics.

A later Ring 8 surface may record the actual Human Gate decision and resulting
authorization state.

R8-B itself does not pre-populate APPROVE.

R8-B itself does not pre-populate REJECT.

R8-B itself does not pre-populate DEFER.

No decision exists until Human Gate actually makes one.

## Invalid Decision Conditions

The following are invalid:

- missing decision actor;
- missing candidate commit;
- missing target branch;
- decision outside APPROVE / REJECT / DEFER;
- inferred Human Gate approval;
- approval bound only to a moving branch name;
- execution claimed by the decision record;
- canonical state claimed before Git proves transition;
- merge claimed before Git proves transition;
- external action authority added by promotion decision;
- system-population authority added by promotion decision.
