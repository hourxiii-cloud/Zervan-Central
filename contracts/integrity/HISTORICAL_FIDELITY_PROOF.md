# AC-08 — Historical Fidelity Proof

Status: ACTIVATION CORRECTION
Priority: HIGH
Version: vTemporal.41.0
Authority: NONE
Human Gate: ACTIVE

## 0. Purpose

AC-08 proves that historical Replay state is attributable to what actually
existed at the claimed historical state.

Historical fidelity != current resolvability.

Replay must prove what existed then.

Equivalent now != attributable then.

A currently resolvable object, question, evidence item, lineage edge,
Collapse record, finding, or rendering MUST NOT be substituted merely because
it is semantically or structurally equivalent to the historical referent.

## 1. Governing Law

A historical referent is admissible only when its identity and required
relationships are provable against the claimed historical state.

Current existence does not prove historical existence.

Current resolution does not prove historical resolution.

Current equivalence does not prove historical identity.

Historical proof MUST be bounded by an attributable historical state root,
manifest/revision state, or other governed immutable historical witness.

## 2. Historical Proof Record

Every Historical Fidelity Proof MUST preserve:

- proof identifier;
- canonical Room Object identity;
- historical Room revision;
- historical State Root;
- historical observational coordinate;
- historical question reference;
- historical inquiry-envelope reference;
- historical evidence-used references;
- historical evidence boundary;
- historical evidence ceiling;
- historical provenance route;
- historical lineage edges;
- historical Collapse references;
- historical eliminated alternatives;
- historical retained alternatives;
- historical deferred alternatives;
- historical findings;
- historical rendering reference or explicit historical absence;
- historical Cartography reference;
- historical Stick reference;
- proof witnesses;
- proof disposition;
- blocking reasons;
- provenance route;
- authority state;
- Human Gate state.

## 3. As-Of Resolution

Every historical referent MUST resolve as-of the claimed historical state.

Resolution against current state alone is insufficient.

A referent that exists now but cannot be proven to have existed then is
historically unproven.

Historically unproven != historically absent.

Failure to prove historical existence MUST remain explicit.

## 4. Provenance Fidelity

Historical provenance MUST preserve the actual historical chain.

AC-08 MUST reject:

- provenance grafting;
- provenance truncation;
- provenance temporal reordering;
- false provenance chains whose fields are syntactically complete;
- current lineage substituted for historical lineage.

Every historical lineage edge MUST be attributable to the historical state in
which that edge is claimed to have existed.

Provenance field exists != provenance chain is true.

## 5. Historical Question Fidelity

Replay MUST preserve the question that existed at the historical coordinate.

The current question MUST NOT substitute for the historical question.

Equivalent question wording does not prove historical question identity.

Historical question proof requires an attributable historical reference.

## 6. Historical Evidence Fidelity

Replay MUST preserve evidence actually admitted and used historically.

Current evidence MUST NOT be rebound into historical Replay.

Later evidence MUST NOT strengthen a historical finding.

A later evidence ceiling MUST NOT retroactively elevate historical claim
strength.

Evidence available now != evidence available then.

Evidence available then != evidence used then.

## 7. Replay / Reflight Separation

Replay preserves historical state.

Reflight may establish a new current analytical movement when separately
governed.

Replay MUST NOT mix historical and current evidence into one historical state.

New evidence belongs to a new attributable analytical operation.

Replay != retrospective re-analysis.

## 8. Collapse Fidelity

Historical Collapse proof MUST preserve:

- Collapse identity;
- trigger;
- governing evidence;
- eliminated alternatives;
- retained alternatives;
- deferred alternatives;
- surviving uncertainty;
- frontier;
- return route.

Restoring the final answer while losing eliminated alternatives is not
historical fidelity.

Historical Collapse != final-answer reconstruction.

## 9. Rendering Fidelity

Where a historical rendering existed, Replay MUST preserve its attributable
historical identity.

Where no historical rendering existed, that absence MUST remain explicit.

A regenerated rendering MUST NOT be represented as the historical original.

Equivalent rendering != historical rendering.

## 10. Proof Disposition

AC-08 defines:

- PROVEN;
- BLOCKED.

PROVEN means every required historical referent and relationship is
attributably bound to the claimed historical state.

BLOCKED means one or more required historical identities, relationships,
temporal bindings, or witnesses cannot be established.

BLOCKED != historically false.

BLOCKED != historically absent.

BLOCKED means historical fidelity has not been proven.

## 11. Fail-Closed Conditions

AC-08 MUST block when:

- historical State Root is missing;
- a required referent resolves only in current state;
- historical existence cannot be proven;
- provenance is grafted;
- provenance is truncated;
- provenance edges are temporally reordered;
- provenance fields exist but the chain is false;
- current evidence is rebound into historical Replay;
- current question substitutes for historical question;
- later evidence strengthens a historical finding;
- Replay and Reflight evidence are mixed;
- Collapse history is mutated;
- eliminated alternatives disappear;
- regenerated rendering is represented as historical;
- required proof witness is missing;
- provenance is missing.

Fail closed.

Do not reconstruct historical truth from current equivalence.

## 12. Authority Boundary

Historical proof creates no execution authority.

Historical proof creates no publication authority.

Historical proof creates no truth authority.

PROVEN != approved.

PROVEN != execute.

Authority remains NONE.

Human Gate remains ACTIVE.
